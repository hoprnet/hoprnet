1//! Turns SURB round-trips into edge telemetry for the network graph.
2//!
3//! A SURB rides a forward path to reach its destination and carries a return path for the reply to
4//! come back on. When the reply arrives, that is proof both legs passed end to end -- evidence the
5//! graph already wants, produced by traffic a session was sending anyway. Unlike a probe it costs
6//! no extra packets and accrues at data rates, which is what lets a dead relayer be noticed in
7//! seconds rather than after a probe success rate has moved behind a path cache.
8//!
9//! # Why one layer owns both directions
10//!
11//! Minting and consuming happen at opposite ends of the pipeline, but only together do they mean
12//! anything: the mint says what was expected, the reply says what was observed. Wrapping both codec
13//! halves keeps the SURB-to-path association private to this module, so nothing below has to carry
14//! a path identity it has no other use for.
15//!
16//! # Mitigations driven from here
17//!
18//! Each names its trigger, its effect, its setting and what it must not work against.
19//!
20//! - **Relayer health.** Trigger: a relayer losing reply races (two SURBs of one packet, different return paths) by
21//!   more than a round trip. Effect: its health in the graph scales every edge into it, so forward and return paths
22//!   through it are drawn less. Settings: `HoprProtocolConfig::latency_race_memory`,
23//!   `HoprProtocolConfig::relayer_health_weight` (`0` off). Must not fight: the return-path temper, which flattens it
24//!   along with path quality.
25//! - **Dead verdict.** Trigger: at least `MIN_RACES` replies trailing their partners by more than any race the relayer
26//!   settled, none home since, and a reply home through some relayer after the first of them fell due. A reply is
27//!   overdue only while others are delivering: a pause in the replier's stream, or an outage of every return relayer,
28//!   leaves nothing arriving and so nothing overdue, however long the replies stay out. A relayer that has settled too
29//!   few races to have a margin of its own borrows the bar of the best-measured relayer, raised to whatever its own
30//!   settled races measure, and none is judged while no relayer has one; its own races that expired open are booked as
31//!   lost and are not replies that came home, so they do not reset the clock. Effect: health `0`, the walk prunes it. A
32//!   verdict is lifted by a reply coming home through the relayer, not by time. From the moment the verdict is acted on
33//!   (`VERDICT_CONFIRMATION`), only a reply on a SURB minted after it counts: the late replies a slow relayer still
34//!   delivers are what the verdict was about, not news that it ended (`RelayerHealth::is_fresh_for`). Once no reply
35//!   through it is out any more (about `PENDING_SURB_TTL`) the condition lapses, and the relayer goes on *probation*:
36//!   published at `PROBATION_HEALTH`, which weighted selection all but never draws while the planner's exploration
37//!   still does, and that draw is the probe. A probe is minted after the verdict, so a probe reply home through it
38//!   revives it, to the health its lost races leave it, so gradually; a probe that goes missing leaves it where it is,
39//!   and one that is a black hole again goes back to dead without a new SURB generation. Must not fight: common-mode
40//!   outages, where no partner gets home and nothing is judged.
41//! - **Invalid SURBs.** Trigger: a dead verdict, or probation. Effect: each session is told how many SURBs in its
42//!   counterparty's store run through that relayer; the balancer subtracts them from its level and sends replacements.
43//!   A lapsed verdict is only a timeout and the counterparty still holds the SURBs, probe SURBs included, so the
44//!   write-off outlives it; each written-off SURB counts as held only until `WRITE_OFF_HORIZON`, the time the
45//!   counterparty needs to spend it, and a reply through the relayer that revives it (one on a SURB minted after the
46//!   verdict, see `RelayerHealth::is_fresh_for`) or the end of the session ends the write-off sooner. A session's count
47//!   is dropped when its SURB generation is advanced, though: the wipe takes the SURBs it counted, and from then on it
48//!   counts only the probes minted since. Must not fight: flow control, which keeps the raw level so the replacements
49//!   are not throttled.
50//! - **Fresh SURB generation.** Trigger: a dead verdict that held for a second flush (`VERDICT_CONFIRMATION`) reaching
51//!   a session for the first time, at most once per (session, relayer) while the relayer is known dead, and per
52//!   `REBUMP_COOLDOWN` after it is revived. Effect: the session's SURB generation advances, so the counterparty drops
53//!   its whole stock on its next push, the dead SURBs it cannot recognise along with the rest, and the balancer refills
54//!   from empty, paced; the session's write-off goes with the stock it counted. Probation is not a new verdict: a pair
55//!   bumped in this death is not bumped again until a reply revives the relayer, so one that dies again on probation
56//!   costs the session nothing more. Only stock advances a generation: a SURB minted after its relayer was already
57//!   known dead is a probe, written off without one, also in a session the wipe never reached. Must not fight: the
58//!   cooldown, since each advance costs the counterparty everything it holds, and a PIX cycle can absorb only so many
59//!   such losses.
60//! - **Lost neighbour.** Trigger: our own connection drops. Effect: re-plan only; the graph's connection status already
61//!   rejects that edge, so its SURBs are not invalidated.

89/// How long a race stays open: a reply still out this long after its partner came home is lost.
90///
91/// Generous relative to a round-trip, because expiring a reply that is merely slow would count a
92/// success as a loss. It also sets the pace of everything measured from a verdict: the lapse into
93/// probation and the re-bump cooldown.
94const PENDING_SURB_TTL: Duration = Duration::from_secs(60);
95
96/// How long a minted SURB stays eligible to be matched with its reply.
97///
98/// Not [`PENDING_SURB_TTL`]: a FIFO replier spends its oldest SURB first, so a reply comes home on a
99/// SURB minted about `stock / reply rate` earlier — 128 s for the deployed 19 264-SURB stock at 150
100/// replies per second, and more for a quiet session. Expiring the association at the race timeout
101/// left every reply of such a session unmatched: no race, no reply evidence, no verdict and no
102/// SURB generation advance, while its Exit spent a dead relayer's SURBs into the void. The map holds
103/// what is outstanding at the counterparty, so its size is bounded by the stock and the loss rate,
104/// not by this horizon; the byte budget in [`pending_surbs_capacity`] bounds it.
105const PENDING_SURB_HORIZON: Duration = Duration::from_secs(15 * 60);
106const _: () = assert!(PENDING_SURB_HORIZON.as_secs() >= 10 * PENDING_SURB_TTL.as_secs());

108/// How long a SURB written off through a relayer known dead keeps counting as held by the
109/// counterparty, and therefore as invalid.
110///
111/// The counterparty spends what it holds within the time it takes its stock to turn over, after
112/// which a written-off SURB is gone and counting it on only makes the balancer over-produce.
113/// Counted for good, the probes through a relayer on probation grew the count without bound
114/// (26 per second at production dimensions: twice the target in 13 minutes, and the balancer at its
115/// ceiling within the hour). Two race timeouts cover the turnover at the production rate (48 s for
116/// the deployed stock at 400 replies a second) and most of it in a quiet Session (128 s at 150);
117/// what a slower Session's count misses is bounded by the probe rate limit, a probe a second.
118const WRITE_OFF_HORIZON: Duration = Duration::from_secs(2 * 60);
119
120/// How long after a relayer is revived, and no longer known dead, the session that was advanced for its
121/// verdict is not advanced again for it: twice [`PENDING_SURB_TTL`], counted from the revival.
122///
123/// While the relayer is known dead, dead or on probation, the pair is never advanced twice, however
124/// long that lasts: the mark of the advance stays until the relayer is revived by a reply, and every
125/// pass that still finds the relayer known dead moves its time on, so that the cooldown starts at the
126/// revival and not at the advance. This is the guard for what follows a revival: a reply that came
127/// home through the relayer ends the verdict, and a relayer that is still dead after all is judged
128/// dead anew, which must not cost the counterparty its whole stock a second time right after the
129/// revival, however long the death before it lasted.
130const REBUMP_COOLDOWN: Duration = Duration::from_secs(2 * 60);
131const _: () = assert!(REBUMP_COOLDOWN.as_secs() == 2 * PENDING_SURB_TTL.as_secs());

133/// The health published for a relayer on probation: a thousandth.
134///
135/// A relayer whose dead verdict has lapsed is not trusted again until a reply that is news about it comes home
136/// through it, one on a SURB minted after the verdict (see `RelayerHealth::is_fresh_for`).
137/// The graph scales every edge into it by this, so weighted selection draws it far less than a healthy
138/// relayer, while the planner's uniform exploration (`return_path_exploration`, 10 % by default) still
139/// draws it as a candidate: that draw is the probe, and a reply on it ends the probation. Not `0`, which
140/// the graph prunes, and nothing would ever probe a pruned relayer. After the `relayer_health_weight`
141/// exponent it stays at a few percent for weights of one half and up (a thousandth to the power one
142/// half is 3 %), and `0` switches it off with the rest of the health.
143const PROBATION_HEALTH: f64 = 1e-3;

145/// How long a dead verdict must have held before it is acted on: the write-off and the SURB generation
146/// advance wait for the next flush to find the relayer still dead.
147///
148/// The flush runs once a second (`surb_flush_interval`). A verdict reaches the graph at once, so the
149/// planner steers around the relayer from the same flush; but a relayer that is merely the slowest in
150/// its session's first seconds — judged against a bar borrowed from the fastest, with no history of its
151/// own — is revived by its own late replies a fraction of a second later, and a bump on that verdict
152/// wipes the counterparty's whole stock for nothing. Measured: one such wipe per ~7 scenario runs, always
153/// at cold start, always the same relayer. Waiting one flush costs a dead relayer one more second of
154/// replies into the void and costs a slow one nothing.
155const VERDICT_CONFIRMATION: Duration = Duration::from_millis(900);

157/// Expected/observed counts for one pair of legs, accumulated between flushes.
158///
159/// Both are monotonic within an interval, so recording is a pair of relaxed atomic adds on the
160/// packet hot path -- no lock, no allocation.
161#[derive(Debug, Default)]
162pub struct SurbRoundTripCounters {
163    expected: AtomicU64,
164    observed: AtomicU64,
165}
166
167impl SurbRoundTripCounters {
168    fn record_expected(&self, count: u64) {
169        self.expected.fetch_add(count, Ordering::Relaxed);
170    }
171
172    fn record_observed(&self, count: u64) {
173        self.observed.fetch_add(count, Ordering::Relaxed);
174    }
175
176    /// Takes both counts, resetting them to zero.
177    fn take(&self) -> (u64, u64) {
178        (
179            self.expected.swap(0, Ordering::Relaxed),
180            self.observed.swap(0, Ordering::Relaxed),
181        )
182    }
183}

185/// Round-trip counts keyed by the legs they were observed over.
186///
187/// Batching is not an optimisation here but a requirement: recording an edge takes a write lock on
188/// the whole graph, a packet carries several SURBs, and a session at 0.5 MB/s moves hundreds of
189/// packets a second -- reporting per event would contend the graph thousands of times a second.
190#[derive(Clone)]
191pub struct SurbRoundTripRegistry {
192    destinations: Arc<DashMap<ForwardAndReturnPath, OffchainPublicKey>>,
193    observer: Option<TransportObserver>,
194    inner: Arc<DashMap<ForwardAndReturnPath, Arc<SurbRoundTripCounters>>>,
195    /// Per-relayer health from reply races, published into the graph.
196    relayer_health: Arc<RelayerHealth>,
197    /// Every outstanding SURB, shared by both codec halves.
198    pending: PendingLegs,
199    /// Per session, SURBs written off because their return path runs through a relayer known dead, by
200    /// that relayer, even though the counterparty may still hold them. Kept while the relayer stays
201    /// known dead (a lapsed verdict only times out, the write-off outlives it), but dropped when the
202    /// session's generation is advanced: the counterparty drops the stock the count stood for, and
203    /// what is counted afterwards is only the SURBs through the relayer minted since, the probes.
204    /// Each entry is the instants its SURBs were written off, oldest first, aged out after
205    /// `WRITE_OFF_HORIZON`; the count is their number.
206    written_off: Arc<DashMap<HoprPseudonym, HashMap<OffchainPublicKey, VecDeque<Instant>>>>,
207    /// When a dead verdict last made [`write_off_dead`](Self::write_off_dead) report a session for a
208    /// relayer, so that the same pair is not reported again. Kept for as long as the relayer is known
209    /// dead, and for `REBUMP_COOLDOWN` after it is not, so a pair advanced in this death stays
210    /// advanced, and a quick re-death after a revival is not advanced again either.
211    bumped: Arc<DashMap<(HoprPseudonym, OffchainPublicKey), Instant>>,
212    /// The return legs of SURBs written off or wiped, for as long as a SURB is remembered.
213    ///
214    /// Writing a SURB off takes it out of `pending`, but the counterparty still holds it and may spend
215    /// it: the reply of a probe through a relayer on probation arrives after the write-off, and a
216    /// wiped stock is spent until the new generation reaches the counterparty. Recognising it here is
217    /// what lets a reply home lift the verdict or the probation of the relayers on its leg. With each leg
218    /// goes the SURB's mint time: a reply on a SURB older than a verdict already acted on is not news
219    /// about that relayer (see `RelayerHealth::is_fresh_for`).
220    written_off_legs: moka::sync::Cache<HoprSurbId, (ReturnRelayers, Instant)>,
221    /// Records either pending map could not hold, counted together: one evicted from
222    /// [`pending`](Self::pending) before its reply could come home settles no race and is never
223    /// written off, and one evicted from `written_off_legs` leaves a reply home finding nothing, so it
224    /// cannot lift its relayer's verdict. Zero in a configuration where neither map fills; non-zero
225    /// means the byte budget is too small for the node's outstanding SURBs, and the metric
226    /// built on [`pending_lost`](Self::pending_lost) says so.
227    pending_lost: Arc<AtomicU64>,
228}

230/// Floor on the byte budget of each pending map: the old fixed 100 000-record cap.
231///
232/// An entry leaves the map when its reply comes home, so what a map holds is the stock outstanding at
233/// the counterparties plus the replies lost within [`PENDING_SURB_HORIZON`]: several deployed-size
234/// stocks. [`pending_surbs_capacity`] sizes the maps from a byte budget, and this floor keeps a budget
235/// below it from shrinking the maps below what they held before that budget existed (hoprnet#8624).
236/// The same figure is exported as
237/// [`MINIMUM_PENDING_SURB_MAX_BYTES`](hopr_protocol_hopr::MINIMUM_PENDING_SURB_MAX_BYTES) and enforced
238/// by the config validator, so a configuration file cannot carry a budget this floor would ignore.
239const MAX_PENDING_SURBS: u64 = 100_000;
240const _: () = assert!(
241    MAX_PENDING_SURBS
242        == hopr_protocol_hopr::MINIMUM_PENDING_SURB_MAX_BYTES / hopr_protocol_hopr::PENDING_SURB_RECORD_BYTES
243);
244
245/// Byte capacity of one pending map, floored so a small budget never shrinks it below
246/// [`MAX_PENDING_SURBS`].
247///
248/// The result is a **byte** figure handed straight to moka's `max_capacity` beside the
249/// [`weigher`](pending_legs) charging one record
250/// [`PENDING_SURB_RECORD_BYTES`](hopr_protocol_hopr::PENDING_SURB_RECORD_BYTES), so the cache's
251/// capacity *is* the budget and no per-record estimate can drift.
252///
253/// Both maps take this same capacity. At the 256 MiB default each holds ~315 700 records (~16
254/// deployed-size stocks) and the pair peaks at ≈512 MiB when both fill — the budget is per map, not
255/// for the node.
256pub(crate) fn pending_surbs_capacity(max_bytes: u64) -> u64 {
257    max_bytes.max(MAX_PENDING_SURBS * PENDING_SURB_RECORD_BYTES)
258}
259
260/// Cost of one remembered SURB, charged to a pending map's weigher so its byte capacity is exact.
261///
262/// Re-exported from the crate that owns the SURB configuration, where the measurement and its
263/// reasoning live; the two must not drift.
264const PENDING_SURB_RECORD_BYTES: u64 = hopr_protocol_hopr::PENDING_SURB_RECORD_BYTES;
265
266impl Default for SurbRoundTripRegistry {
267    fn default() -> Self {
268        // The floor budget, in bytes: the old 100 000-record cap.
269        Self::with_capacity(MAX_PENDING_SURBS * PENDING_SURB_RECORD_BYTES)
270    }
271}

288    /// Notify the optional observer about minted SURBs independently of graph telemetry drains.
289    fn record_minted(&self, paths: ForwardAndReturnPath, count: u64, destination: OffchainPublicKey) {
290        self.destinations.insert(paths, destination);
291        self.record_expected(paths, count);
292        if let Some(observer) = &self.observer {

297                returned: 0,
298            });
299        }

302    /// A registry whose pending maps each hold up to `max_pending` **bytes**.
303    ///
304    /// A byte capacity, not a record count: each map charges one record `PENDING_SURB_RECORD_BYTES`
305    /// through its weigher, so this is the memory bound itself. Production derives it from
306    /// `surb_store.pending_surb_max_bytes` via `pending_surbs_capacity`; [`Default`] passes the floor,
307    /// `MAX_PENDING_SURBS × PENDING_SURB_RECORD_BYTES`.
308    pub fn with_capacity(max_pending: u64) -> Self {
309        let pending_lost = Arc::new(AtomicU64::new(0));
310        let relayer_health = Arc::new(RelayerHealth::default());
311        Self {
312            inner: Default::default(),
313            destinations: Default::default(),
314            observer: None,
315            pending: pending_legs(max_pending, pending_lost.clone(), relayer_health.clone()),
316            relayer_health,
317            written_off: Default::default(),
318            bumped: Default::default(),
319            // The same figure as `pending`, never a separate one: a SURB written off leaves `pending`
320            // for this map, and the two held one number before they were derived from the config. It is
321            // charged the same per-record weight even though its value is smaller, so the pair's worst
322            // case is twice the budget, not 1.5x.
323            //
324            // `lru` for the same reason as `pending`: a written-off leg is looked up once, when the
325            // reply of a SURB the counterparty still held comes home, so TinyLFU would refuse the
326            // newest leg and lose the evidence that revives its relayer. A `Size` eviction is counted
327            // like one on `pending`: the reply then finds the leg in neither map and cannot lift the
328            // verdict or probation of the relayers on it, which is the same loss the metric reports.
329            written_off_legs: moka::sync::Cache::builder()
330                .max_capacity(max_pending)
331                .weigher(|_, _| PENDING_SURB_RECORD_BYTES as u32)
332                .time_to_live(PENDING_SURB_HORIZON)
333                .eviction_policy(moka::policy::EvictionPolicy::lru())
334                .eviction_listener(size_evictions(pending_lost.clone()))
335                .build(),
336            pending_lost,
337        }
338    }

340    /// Records that minted SURBs left one of the two pending maps before their replies could come home,
341    /// because the map was full. A record evicted from `pending` settles no race and is never written
342    /// off; one evicted from the written-off-legs map lets a reply home find nothing and so fail to
343    /// lift the verdict or probation of its relayers. Both are counted here, one series for both maps:
344    /// the two are sized from one budget and fill for the same reason, and what an operator acts on is
345    /// "the pending maps are undersized", not which of the pair overflowed first. The loss is invisible
346    /// in every other counter; non-zero only when the budget is too small for what the node mints. See
347    /// `pending_surbs_capacity`.
348    pub fn pending_lost(&self) -> u64 {
349        self.pending_lost.load(Ordering::Relaxed)
350    }
351
352    /// Whether the reply of the SURB at `pair_index` in its packet says anything about its return path
353    /// when it races its partner's: only the SURB the replier spends first does, see
354    /// [`FIRST_SPENT_INDEX`].
355    ///
356    /// The other one is spent after it, so its reply trails the first's by however long the replier
357    /// took to get to it, a pause in its reply stream included, and that says nothing about a relayer.
358    /// A race therefore opens only in the abnormal order, the later-spent SURB home while the
359    /// first-spent one is still out, and what it measures is lateness down to the return path. Judging
360    /// both would settle every pair in the normal order, and the largest inter-reply gap the replier
361    /// ever had would become the bar a reply must stay out longer than to count as overdue.
362    fn judges(&self, pair_index: usize) -> bool {
363        pair_index == FIRST_SPENT_INDEX
364    }
365
366    /// Opens the race of `partner`, still out over `waiting`, against the reply that just came home over
367    /// `home`, and keeps it open only while `partner` can still close it.
368    ///
369    /// The race is opened after `partner`'s record was updated, so the record can leave the pending map
370    /// in between: home, its reply settling a race not yet open, or evicted, its eviction listener
371    /// finding no race to discard. Either way nothing would ever close the race, and it would expire as a
372    /// loss of the relayers on `waiting`. Checked after the race is opened, so an eviction after the
373    /// check finds the race and discards it itself. A record written off in between is still in
374    /// `written_off_legs` and keeps its race, as one written off after the race opened does.
375    fn open_race(&self, waiting: &ReturnRelayers, partner: HoprSurbId, home: &ReturnRelayers, round_trip: Duration) {
376        self.relayer_health.open_race(waiting, partner, home, round_trip);
377        if !self.pending.contains_key(&partner) && !self.written_off_legs.contains_key(&partner) {
378            self.relayer_health.discard_race(waiting, &partner);
379        }
380    }

396    /// Moves outstanding SURBs whose return path runs through a relayer judged dead, or on probation
397    /// after such a verdict, into the per-session write-off, and drops the write-off of relayers that
398    /// recovered.
399    ///
400    /// Only the SURBs through the relayer are written off, and only they are counted: the
401    /// counterparty's other SURBs are as good as ever until the caller supersedes the whole stock.
402    /// A relayer on probation is known dead all the same: the probes the planner's exploration sends
403    /// through it are written off like any other SURB through it.
404    ///
405    /// Returns the sessions a dead verdict reached for the first time, i.e. the ones for which a
406    /// relayer has SURBs outstanding that were not written off before. The counterparty cannot tell
407    /// which of the SURBs it holds run through the dead relayer, since it sees only their first hop,
408    /// so the caller advances each such session's SURB generation and the counterparty drops its
409    /// whole stock on the next push. The write-off of such a session is dropped at the end of the pass:
410    /// the wipe takes exactly the SURBs it counted, and the balancer's reset and paced refill replace
411    /// them, so counting them on would only make the balancer produce that many SURBs above the target
412    /// for good. The SURBs through the relayer minted after the wipe are written off anew on later
413    /// passes, and the count stays small and current.
414    ///
415    /// A verdict is acted on only once it has held for `VERDICT_CONFIRMATION`: a relayer judged dead in
416    /// this flush is written off and bumped at the next one, if it is still dead then.
417    ///
418    /// Only stock reports a session: a SURB minted through the relayer before it was judged dead, which
419    /// the counterparty may hold. One minted after is a probe the planner's exploration sent through a
420    /// relayer published at zero or on probation, and is written off without a report. That holds in
421    /// a session the wipe never reached too, one opened since or with nothing through the relayer at
422    /// the time, which has no mark for the pair: its first probe must not cost it its stock.
423    /// A (session, relayer) pair is reported once while the relayer is known dead, and after a reply
424    /// revives it once per `REBUMP_COOLDOWN` from the revival: a verdict reached again inside either
425    /// is written off as usual but reports nothing.
426    pub fn write_off_dead(&self) -> Vec<HoprPseudonym> {
427        let health = &self.relayer_health;
428        for mut session in self.written_off.iter_mut() {
429            session.retain(|relayer, written_off_at| {
430                // Spent by the counterparty since, and gone with it: see `WRITE_OFF_HORIZON`.
431                while written_off_at
432                    .front()
433                    .is_some_and(|at| at.elapsed() > WRITE_OFF_HORIZON)
434                {
435                    written_off_at.pop_front();
436                }
437                health.is_known_dead(relayer) && !written_off_at.is_empty()
438            });
439        }
440        self.written_off.retain(|_, session| !session.is_empty());
441        // A mark stays while its relayer is known dead, its time moved on by every such pass, so that
442        // `REBUMP_COOLDOWN` runs from the revival however long the death lasted.
443        let pass = Instant::now();
444        self.bumped.retain(|(_, relayer), at| {
445            if health.is_known_dead(relayer) {
446                *at = pass;
447                true
448            } else {
449                at.elapsed() < REBUMP_COOLDOWN
450            }
451        });
452        if health.dead.is_empty() && health.probation.is_empty() {
453            return Vec::new();
454        }
455        // Only verdicts that held for a second flush act on the stock; see `VERDICT_CONFIRMATION`.
456        #[allow(clippy::type_complexity)]
457        let through_dead: Vec<(HoprSurbId, HoprPseudonym, OffchainPublicKey, ReturnRelayers, Instant)> = self
458            .pending
459            .iter()
460            .filter_map(|(id, pending)| {
461                relayers_of(&pending.return_relayers)
462                    .find(|relayer| health.is_confirmed_dead(relayer))
463                    .map(|dead| (*id, pending.pseudonym, dead, pending.return_relayers, pending.minted_at))
464            })
465            .collect();
466        let mut reached: Vec<HoprPseudonym> = Vec::new();
467        for (id, pseudonym, dead, return_relayers, minted_at) in through_dead {
468            // Remembered before it leaves `pending`, so that a reply decoded in between finds it in
469            // one of the two.
470            self.written_off_legs.insert(id, (return_relayers, minted_at));
471            self.pending.invalidate(&id);
472            let mut session = self.written_off.entry(pseudonym).or_default();
473            let first_for_pair = !session.contains_key(&dead);
474            session.entry(dead).or_default().push_back(Instant::now());
475            drop(session);
476            // A probe, not stock: see the docs above. Judged per SURB, and before the mark, so that a
477            // probe written off first leaves the stock SURBs of the same pass free to report the pair.
478            if health.known_dead_since(&dead).is_some_and(|since| minted_at >= since) {
479                tracing::trace!(
480                    %pseudonym,
481                    relayer = %dead,
482                    "probe through a relayer known dead, written off without a new SURB generation"
483                );
484                continue;
485            }
486            if self.bumped.contains_key(&(pseudonym, dead)) {
487                if first_for_pair {
488                    tracing::debug!(
489                        %pseudonym,
490                        relayer = %dead,
491                        "SURB through a relayer already bumped for, written off without a new SURB generation"
492                    );
493                }
494                continue;
495            }
496            self.bumped.insert((pseudonym, dead), Instant::now());
497            if !reached.contains(&pseudonym) {
498                reached.push(pseudonym);
499            }
500        }
501        // The wipe takes what was counted, whatever else was counted for the session in this pass.
502        for pseudonym in &reached {
503            self.written_off.remove(pseudonym);
504        }
505        if !reached.is_empty() {
506            // The wipe takes the session's whole stock, so it settles every relayer known dead now,
507            // not only the one each pending SURB was found under: a SURB names the first confirmed dead
508            // relayer on its path, and one judged in this same flush is not confirmed yet. Left out,
509            // such a relayer turns the first SURB through it after the wipe into a pair no wipe has
510            // covered, and that SURB is an exploring probe once the relayer is on probation: the
511            // generation advanced again for it, and the counterparty dropped a fresh stock of thousands
512            // over one SURB. SURBs through these relayers from now on are written off, as through any
513            // relayer already bumped for; a relayer that dies after this wipe still gets its own.
514            let now = Instant::now();
515            let known_dead: Vec<OffchainPublicKey> = health
516                .dead
517                .iter()
518                .map(|relayer| *relayer)
519                .chain(health.probation.iter().map(|relayer| *relayer))
520                .collect();
521            for pseudonym in &reached {
522                for relayer in &known_dead {
523                    self.bumped.entry((*pseudonym, *relayer)).or_insert(now);
524                }
525            }
526
527            // The counterparty drops its whole stock on the first batch of the new generation, but it
528            // goes on spending that stock until the batch arrives. Taken out of `pending`, these SURBs
529            // cannot make a second relayer confirmed at a later flush look as if it still had a stock
530            // to wipe. Their legs are kept as written-off legs are, before they leave `pending`, so
531            // that a reply spent in the meantime still proves its relayers alive: it can revive one
532            // judged dead in this same flush, whose verdict is not confirmed yet.
533            let wiped: Vec<(HoprSurbId, ReturnRelayers, Instant)> = self
534                .pending
535                .iter()
536                .filter(|(_, pending)| reached.contains(&pending.pseudonym))
537                .map(|(id, pending)| (*id, pending.return_relayers, pending.minted_at))
538                .collect();
539            for (id, return_relayers, minted_at) in wiped {
540                self.written_off_legs.insert(id, (return_relayers, minted_at));
541                self.pending.invalidate(&id);
542            }
543        }
544        reached
545    }
546
547    /// Per session, how many SURBs held by the counterparty cannot bring a reply home, and their share
548    /// of all it was handed that are still accounted for.
549    ///
550    /// Walks the whole pending map to count what is outstanding per session, as does
551    /// [`write_off_dead`](Self::write_off_dead), which walks it twice. Both run once per flush, this one
552    /// only when some session has written-off SURBs and the second only during a dead-relayer verdict,
553    /// so at the 256 MiB default the walk is over up to ~315 700 records: measured at ~500 ns per entry,
554    /// about 160 ms per pass (hoprnet#8624). The flush therefore runs both on the blocking pool, not on
555    /// a runtime worker and not on the packet hot path.
556    pub fn invalid(&self) -> HashMap<HoprPseudonym, (u64, f64)> {
557        if self.written_off.is_empty() {
558            return HashMap::new();
559        }
560        let mut outstanding: HashMap<HoprPseudonym, u64> = HashMap::new();
561        for (_, pending) in self.pending.iter() {
562            *outstanding.entry(pending.pseudonym).or_default() += 1;
563        }
564        self.written_off
565            .iter()
566            .map(|session| {
567                let count: u64 = session.values().map(|written_off_at| written_off_at.len() as u64).sum();
568                let total = count + outstanding.get(session.key()).copied().unwrap_or_default();
569                (*session.key(), (count, count as f64 / total.max(1) as f64))
570            })
571            .collect()
572    }
573
574    /// Drops the write-off and the re-bump cooldown of a session that no longer exists.
575    pub fn forget_session(&self, pseudonym: &HoprPseudonym) {
576        self.written_off.remove(pseudonym);
577        self.bumped.retain(|(session, _), _| session != pseudonym);
578    }
579
580    /// Records that `count` replies arrived over these legs.
581    pub fn record_observed(&self, paths: ForwardAndReturnPath, count: u64) {
582        self.counters(paths).record_observed(count);
583        if let Some(observer) = &self.observer {
584            let destination = self.destinations.get(&paths).map(|destination| *destination);
585            if let Some(destination) = destination {
586                observer(TransportEvent::Surb {
587                    destination,
588                    paths,
589                    minted: 0,
590                    returned: count,
591                });
592            }
593        }
594    }
595
596    /// Takes every non-empty entry, resetting the counts.
597    pub fn drain(&self) -> Vec<(ForwardAndReturnPath, u64, u64)> {
598        self.inner
599            .iter()
600            .filter_map(|entry| {
601                let (expected, observed) = entry.value().take();
602                (expected > 0 || observed > 0).then(|| (*entry.key(), expected, observed))
603            })
604            .collect()
605    }

626/// What a minted SURB is remembered by until its reply comes home.
627#[derive(Debug, Clone, Copy)]
628pub(crate) struct PendingSurb {

630    paths: ForwardAndReturnPath,
631    /// The session it was minted for.
632    pseudonym: HoprPseudonym,
633    /// Relayers the reply passes, any of which a late or missing reply implicates.
634    return_relayers: ReturnRelayers,

636    minted_at: Instant,
637    /// The SURB minted in the same packet over another return path, which this one races.
638    partner: Option<HoprSurbId>,
639    /// Its position in the pair, `0` or `1`; see [`SurbRoundTripRegistry::judges`].
640    pair_index: usize,

645// The byte budget is charged per record from `PENDING_SURB_RECORD_BYTES`, a measurement of this
646// struct plus moka's overhead. Growing the struct would silently exceed the budget, so it forces a
647// re-measurement instead.
648const _: () = assert!(
649    size_of::<PendingSurb>() <= 504,
650    "PendingSurb grew: re-measure PENDING_SURB_RECORD_BYTES and the pair's worst case"
651);

653/// What each outstanding SURB is remembered by, shared between the two codec halves.
654///
655/// Minting happens on the encoder and the reply arrives at the decoder, so this **must** be one map
656/// shared by both. Giving each half its own leaves every lookup missing and silently discards the
657/// entire observation side of the metric.
658type PendingLegs = moka::sync::Cache<HoprSurbId, PendingSurb>;
659
660/// Builds the shared pending map, bounded by a byte capacity, a per-record weight and a TTL.
661///
662/// `max_capacity` is in **bytes**, matched by the [`weigher`](moka::sync::CacheBuilder::weigher) that
663/// charges every record `PENDING_SURB_RECORD_BYTES`, so the budget is the map's real bound.
664///
665/// Admission is `lru`, not moka's default TinyLFU: a SURB is looked up once, when its reply comes home,
666/// so every key's frequency is about one and TinyLFU refuses the newest record once the map is full. A
667/// refused record settles no race and, if the SURB is lost, is never written off (hoprnet#8624).
668///
669/// Under `lru` the newest is always admitted and the victim is the oldest, whose reply comes next under
670/// FIFO — the cost the issue names. It is bounded by the budget while outstanding SURBs fit: a record
671/// survives `capacity / mint rate`, and a reply comes home after the counterparty has spent its stock
672/// (minutes, which is why [`PENDING_SURB_HORIZON`] is 15 minutes). Once the outstanding total exceeds
673/// the capacity — above ~16 fully-buffered Sessions at the default — residency falls below that wait, so
674/// an ever larger share of records is evicted before their replies arrive, and one high-rate Session
675/// shortens the residence time for every other. That regime is reachable by design and is counted via
676/// [`SurbRoundTripRegistry::pending_lost`]; raising `pending_surb_max_bytes` extends the range over
677/// which eviction is not the normal state.
678///
679/// A record evicted while its partner is home leaves the race that partner opened with nothing to
680/// close it: the reply finds no record, settles nothing, and the race would expire as a loss of the
681/// relayers on its leg, healthy or not. Under sustained eviction that is enough unanswered races to
682/// judge a healthy relayer dead and wipe its Sessions' stock. An eviction is no evidence about a
683/// relayer either way, so the race is discarded with the record (see
684/// [`SurbRoundTripRegistry::open_race`] for the eviction that lands before the race opens).
685fn pending_legs(max_pending: u64, lost: Arc<AtomicU64>, health: Arc<RelayerHealth>) -> PendingLegs {
686    let count_lost = size_evictions(lost);
687    moka::sync::Cache::builder()
688        .max_capacity(max_pending)
689        .weigher(|_, _| PENDING_SURB_RECORD_BYTES as u32)
690        .time_to_live(PENDING_SURB_HORIZON)
691        .eviction_policy(moka::policy::EvictionPolicy::lru())
692        // Runs on whichever thread's read or write triggers moka's housekeeping, and takes
693        // `RelayerHealth`'s per-relayer locks, so `pending` must never be used while one is held. It
694        // holds by construction: `RelayerHealth` has no handle on `pending`.
695        .eviction_listener(move |surb, pending: PendingSurb, cause| {
696            if cause == moka::notification::RemovalCause::Size && pending.partner_replied.is_some() {
697                health.discard_race(&pending.return_relayers, &surb);
698            }
699            count_lost(surb, pending, cause);
700        })
701        .build()
702}
703
704/// An eviction listener counting only [`RemovalCause::Size`](moka::notification::RemovalCause::Size)
705/// into `lost`, shared by both pending maps.
706///
707/// Expiry is the horizon doing its job, not a loss, and `Replaced` is an update of a record already
708/// accounted for; only `Size` is a record a map could not hold. `Explicit` is not counted either:
709/// `pending` is consumed with `remove` and pruned with `invalidate`, both of which moka reports as
710/// `Explicit`, so a write-off or a reply leaving the map is not a loss.
711fn size_evictions<V>(
712    lost: Arc<AtomicU64>,
713) -> impl Fn(std::sync::Arc<HoprSurbId>, V, moka::notification::RemovalCause) + Send + Sync + 'static
714where
715    V: Send + Sync + 'static,
716{
717    move |_, _, cause| {
718        if cause == moka::notification::RemovalCause::Size {
719            lost.fetch_add(1, Ordering::Relaxed);
720        }
721    }
722}

801#[derive(Debug, Default)]
802struct RaceHistory {
803    races: VecDeque<Race>,
804    /// Races whose partner is home while this relayer's reply is not, by the SURB still out. Each
805    /// counts as lost by at least the time since the partner came home.
806    open: HashMap<HoprSurbId, Race>,
807    /// How far back its races count, set when it is judged; zero until then.
808    memory: Duration,
809    /// When a reply last came home through this relayer, whether or not it settled a race.
810    last_home: Option<Instant>,
811}
812
813/// The fewest races whose median no single race decides.
814const MIN_RACES: usize = 3;
815
816impl RaceHistory {
817    fn forget_older_than(&mut self, memory: Duration) {
818        while self.races.len() > MIN_RACES && self.races.front().is_some_and(|race| race.at.elapsed() > memory) {
819            self.races.pop_front();
820        }
821    }
822
823    /// The races that settled, as opposed to those that expired open and were booked as lost.
824    fn finished(&self) -> impl Iterator<Item = &Race> {
825        self.races.iter().filter(|race| !race.lost())
826    }
827
828    /// How late a reply of this relayer usually is: the largest margin of its settled races, or `None`
829    /// until it has settled [`MIN_RACES`] of them, too few for the figure to mean anything.
830    fn usual_margin(&self) -> Option<f64> {
831        (self.finished().count() >= MIN_RACES)
832            .then(|| self.finished().map(|race| race.margin.abs()).fold(0.0, f64::max))
833    }
834
835    /// A black hole: at least [`MIN_RACES`] replies through it are out for longer than its bar, and
836    /// nothing has come home through it since each of them fell due. The bar is the largest margin of
837    /// the races it finished, or, while it has finished too few, the borrowed one below.
838    ///
839    /// A reply is overdue only if a reply came home, through any relayer, after its grace ran out
840    /// (`last_reply`): the network was delivering while this relayer was not. Without that, nothing
841    /// tells a dead relayer from a pause. The replier's stream stops for a settle, a phase change or
842    /// idle, nothing is home for anybody during it, and a few replies lost at random before it would
843    /// pass for a verdict, each costing the session its whole stock. An outage of every return
844    /// relayer is likewise no verdict on any one of them. When the stream resumes an alive relayer
845    /// settles a race, which clears the condition below, while a dead one never does.
846    ///
847    /// A relayer that has settled fewer than [`MIN_RACES`] races has no margin of its own to go by,
848    /// and races settle only in the abnormal order, so a clean history is common. It borrows
849    /// `fallback_usual`, the bar of the best-measured relayer, raised to whatever its own settled races
850    /// measure (`own.max(fallback)`), and is not judged at all while no relayer has one. "Home through
851    /// it" is a race it settled or any reply over a leg it is on; the relayer's own races that expired
852    /// open and were booked as lost are not replies that came home, and must not reset the clock.
853    ///
854    /// Each overdue race is judged on its own: one that a reply through the relayer came home after is
855    /// answered, the relayer was delivering after the reply it lost, which was then lost at random.
856    /// Comparing the latest reply with the *oldest* overdue race instead lets one stale race, or the
857    /// replies already past the relayer when it died, hold a verdict back until that race expires, up
858    /// to a minute.
859    ///
860    /// Returns those replies, by SURB, empty while the relayer cannot be judged at all; whether they
861    /// make it a black hole is up to [`explain_away`], which sets aside the ones a relayer already
862    /// judged dead explains.
863    fn unanswered(&self, last_reply: Option<Instant>, fallback_usual: Option<f64>) -> Vec<HoprSurbId> {
864        let own = self.finished().map(|race| race.margin.abs()).fold(0.0, f64::max);
865        // No round-trip floor: a round trip includes the time the SURB waited in the replier's store,
866        // seconds to a minute after a drain, while both SURBs of a pair wait alike, so margins don't.
867        let usual = match (self.usual_margin(), fallback_usual) {
868            (Some(usual), _) => usual,
869            (None, Some(fallback)) => own.max(fallback),
870            (None, None) => return Vec::new(),
871        };
872        let grace = Duration::from_secs_f64(usual);
873        // Home through it: a race it settled, or any reply through it. A reply in the normal order
874        // settles no race, but it is the relayer delivering all the same; counting settled races alone
875        // undid the revival such a reply makes at the very next flush.
876        let latest_home = self.finished().map(|race| race.at).max().max(self.last_home);
877        let now = Instant::now();
878        self.open
879            .iter()
880            .filter_map(|(surb, race)| race.at.checked_add(grace).map(|due| (*surb, due)))
881            // Due, and overdue while others delivered (see above)...
882            .filter(|(_, due)| *due < now && last_reply.is_some_and(|reply| reply > *due))
883            // ...and nothing home through it since it fell due.
884            .filter(|(_, due)| latest_home.is_none_or(|home| home < *due))
885            .map(|(surb, _)| surb)
886            .collect()
887    }
888}

890/// Of the relayers whose overdue replies would make them black holes, those whose lateness no other
891/// one explains.
892///
893/// A reply out late counts against every relayer on its leg that its partner's leg does not share,
894/// since a race cannot tell which of them held it. With a leg's relayers drawn from many, the
895/// relayers on legs through a dead one collected its overdue replies too, and were judged dead with
896/// it: on a cluster, an innocent relayer was judged dead in two of every three runs that killed one
897/// (48 of 72) once the paths were spread over every first relayer, against one in four before.
898///
899/// A dead relayer is on all the legs that failed through it, an innocent one only on those it
900/// shares with it, so the dead one has the most. They are taken in that order: one is a black hole
901/// if at least [`MIN_RACES`] of its unanswered replies are on legs through no relayer already taken,
902/// and every reply out on a leg through it is then explained. Two relayers dead at once are both
903/// taken while each has replies the other does not explain; one that only ever sat on legs through
904/// the other cannot be told from an innocent one, and waits until those legs are gone.
905///
906/// The fewest relayers that explain the overdue replies are taken, so the reverse also holds: a live
907/// relayer whose every leg ran through relayers that died collects all their overdue replies, more
908/// than any of them has, and is taken in their place, one dead relayer explaining the lateness as well
909/// as two. That takes a live relayer with nothing delivered through it since: one that also sits on a
910/// leg that delivers has its overdue replies answered (see [`RaceHistory::unanswered`]) and is no
911/// suspect. Ties of equal counts go by key.
912fn explain_away(
913    mut suspects: Vec<(OffchainPublicKey, Vec<HoprSurbId>, Vec<HoprSurbId>)>,
914) -> std::collections::HashSet<OffchainPublicKey> {
915    suspects.sort_by_cached_key(|(relayer, unanswered, _)| (std::cmp::Reverse(unanswered.len()), relayer.to_string()));
916    let mut explained: std::collections::HashSet<HoprSurbId> = std::collections::HashSet::new();
917    let mut black_holes = std::collections::HashSet::new();
918    for (relayer, unanswered, open) in suspects {
919        let own = unanswered.iter().filter(|surb| !explained.contains(*surb)).count();
920        if own >= MIN_RACES {
921            black_holes.insert(relayer);
922            explained.extend(open);
923        } else {
924            tracing::debug!(
925                %relayer,
926                unanswered = unanswered.len(),
927                own,
928                "overdue replies explained by a relayer judged dead on their legs"
929            );
930        }
931    }
932    black_holes
933}

935/// A relayer's standing in its races: median margin behind its partners, that median's
936/// uncertainty, and the fastest round-trip among them.
937#[derive(Debug, Clone, Copy)]
938struct Judgement {
939    margin: f64,
940    uncertainty: f64,
941    fastest: f64,
942}
943
944impl Judgement {
945    /// Health from the relayer's lateness, `unit` being the fastest round-trip any relayer made:
946    /// lagging by less than that costs nothing.
947    fn health(&self, unit: f64) -> f64 {
948        unit / unit.max(self.margin - self.uncertainty)
949    }
950
951    /// The healths its races cannot tell apart: lateness within its uncertainty, or within a
952    /// round-trip, which healthy relayers differ by anyway.
953    fn indistinct(&self, unit: f64) -> std::ops::RangeInclusive<f64> {
954        let slack = self.uncertainty.max(unit);
955        unit / unit.max(self.margin + slack)..=unit / unit.max(self.margin - slack)
956    }
957}

971        winning_round_trip: Duration,
972        by: Duration,

1107    /// Whether a reply on a SURB minted at `minted_at` is news about `relayer`. It is, unless the
1108    /// relayer's verdict was acted on (confirmed, or the relayer is on probation) and the SURB is older

1113    /// its own late replies without costing a wipe, and a probe minted after the verdict always counts.

1119    /// delivering: any that was judged dead or on probation is alive again, unless the reply is not news
1120    /// about it (see [`Self::is_fresh_for`]). Its health is then what its races say, which still holds
1121    /// the ones it lost, so it recovers gradually as they are forgotten.
1122    fn revive_by_reply(&self, leg: &ReturnRelayers, minted_at: Instant) {
1123        for relayer in relayers_of(leg) {
1124            // Looked up before it is taken out: this is on the path of every reply, and the sets are
1125            // empty nearly always.
1126            if !self.is_known_dead(&relayer) || !self.is_fresh_for(&relayer, minted_at) {
1127                continue;
1128            }
1129            let was_dead = self.dead.remove(&relayer).is_some();
1130            self.dead_since.remove(&relayer);
1131            self.known_dead_since.remove(&relayer);
1132            self.confirmed.remove(&relayer);
1133            let was_on_probation = self.probation.remove(&relayer).is_some();
1134            if was_dead || was_on_probation {
1135                tracing::info!(%relayer, on_probation = was_on_probation, "relayer revived by a reply");
1136            }
1137        }
1138    }

1150    /// Re-judges every relayer once per flush; returns `(relayer, previous, current)` health changes for
1151    /// the graph, dead being `0.0` and probation `PROBATION_HEALTH`. A race open longer than a SURB is
1152    /// remembered counts as lost by that long. `memory`: margins a race is remembered for, `0` for dead,
1153    /// on probation or healthy.
1154    ///
1155    /// A relayer is alive until it is a black hole, which makes it dead. Its verdict does not end with the
1156    /// condition: once no overdue reply is out any more it goes on probation instead, and stays there
1157    /// until a reply on a SURB minted after the verdict comes home through it (`revive_by_reply`,
1158    /// `is_fresh_for`), a probe's above all. One on probation that is a black hole again is dead again.
1159    pub fn refresh(&self, memory: u32) -> Vec<(OffchainPublicKey, f64, f64)> {
1160        let last_reply = self.last_reply();
1161        // The bar of the best-measured relayer, for those with too short a history of their own.
1162        let fallback_usual = self
1163            .relayers
1164            .iter()
1165            .filter_map(|entry| entry.usual_margin())
1166            .max_by(f64::total_cmp);
1167        // Relayers whose overdue replies would make them black holes: their unanswered replies, and every
1168        // reply still out on a leg through them.
1169        let mut suspects: Vec<(OffchainPublicKey, Vec<HoprSurbId>, Vec<HoprSurbId>)> = Vec::new();
1170        for mut entry in self.relayers.iter_mut() {
1171            // A race open past the time a SURB is remembered is lost, by that long: the relayer keeps
1172            // a health that low until it proves otherwise, rather than being forgiven by expiry.
1173            let mut lost = Vec::new();
1174            entry.open.retain(|_, race| {
1175                let open = race.at.elapsed() <= PENDING_SURB_TTL;
1176                if !open {
1177                    lost.push(Race {
1178                        at: Instant::now(),
1179                        margin: PENDING_SURB_TTL.as_secs_f64(),
1180                        ..*race
1181                    });
1182                }
1183                open
1184            });
1185            entry.races.extend(lost);
1186            let unanswered = entry.unanswered(last_reply, fallback_usual);
1187            if unanswered.len() >= MIN_RACES {
1188                suspects.push((*entry.key(), unanswered, entry.open.keys().copied().collect()));
1189            }
1190        }
1191        let black_holes = explain_away(suspects);
1192        self.dead.retain(|relayer| {
1193            let still = black_holes.contains(relayer);
1194            if still {
1195                // Still standing at a flush `VERDICT_CONFIRMATION` after it: from now on it is acted on.
1196                if self
1197                    .dead_since
1198                    .get(relayer)
1199                    .is_some_and(|since| since.elapsed() >= VERDICT_CONFIRMATION)
1200                {
1201                    self.confirmed.insert(*relayer);
1202                }
1203            } else if self.confirmed.remove(relayer).is_some() {
1204                tracing::info!(%relayer, "dead verdict lapsed into probation");
1205                self.dead_since.remove(relayer);
1206                self.probation.insert(*relayer);
1207            } else {
1208                // Gone before the second look: the verdict never held, and nothing was written off or
1209                // advanced for it, so there is nothing to probe either.
1210                tracing::info!(%relayer, "dead verdict withdrawn before it was confirmed");
1211                self.dead_since.remove(relayer);
1212                self.known_dead_since.remove(relayer);
1213            }
1214            still
1215        });
1216        let back_from_probation: Vec<OffchainPublicKey> = self
1217            .probation
1218            .iter()
1219            .filter(|relayer| black_holes.contains(relayer))
1220            .map(|relayer| *relayer)
1221            .collect();
1222        for relayer in back_from_probation {
1223            tracing::info!(%relayer, "relayer on probation judged dead again");
1224            self.probation.remove(&relayer);
1225            // Probation is a standing verdict already: no second flush to wait for.
1226            self.dead.insert(relayer);
1227            self.confirmed.insert(relayer);
1228            self.dead_since.insert(
1229                relayer,
1230                Instant::now()
1231                    .checked_sub(VERDICT_CONFIRMATION)
1232                    .unwrap_or_else(Instant::now),
1233            );
1234        }
1235        for relayer in black_holes {
1236            if !self.is_known_dead(&relayer) {
1237                tracing::info!(%relayer, "relayer judged dead");
1238                self.mark_dead(relayer);
1239            }
1240        }
1241
1242        let judged: HashMap<OffchainPublicKey, Judgement> = self
1243            .relayers
1244            .iter()
1245            .map(|entry| *entry.key())
1246            .collect::<Vec<_>>()
1247            .into_iter()
1248            .filter_map(|relayer| self.judge(&relayer, memory).map(|judgement| (relayer, judgement)))
1249            .collect();
1250        // A relayer gone from the paths races no more; its history only grows the map.
1251        self.relayers.retain(|_, history| {
1252            !history.open.is_empty()
1253                || history
1254                    .races
1255                    .back()
1256                    .is_some_and(|race| race.at.elapsed() <= history.memory.max(PENDING_SURB_TTL))
1257        });
1258        let unit = judged
1259            .values()
1260            .map(|judgement| judgement.fastest)
1261            .min_by(f64::total_cmp);
1262
1263        let relayers: std::collections::HashSet<OffchainPublicKey> = judged
1264            .keys()
1265            .copied()
1266            .chain(self.published.iter().map(|entry| *entry.key()))
1267            .chain(self.dead.iter().map(|relayer| *relayer))
1268            .chain(self.probation.iter().map(|relayer| *relayer))
1269            .collect();
1270
1271        let mut changed = Vec::new();
1272        for relayer in relayers {
1273            let previous = self.published.get(&relayer).map_or(1.0, |health| *health);
1274            let current = match (judged.get(&relayer), unit) {
1275                _ if self.dead.contains(&relayer) => 0.0,
1276                _ if self.probation.contains(&relayer) => PROBATION_HEALTH,
1277                (Some(judgement), Some(unit)) if memory > 0 => {
1278                    if previous > 0.0 && judgement.indistinct(unit).contains(&previous) {
1279                        continue;
1280                    }
1281                    judgement.health(unit)
1282                }
1283                _ => 1.0,
1284            };
1285            if current == previous {
1286                continue;
1287            }
1288            if current < 1.0 {
1289                self.published.insert(relayer, current);
1290            } else {
1291                self.published.remove(&relayer);
1292            }
1293            changed.push((relayer, previous, current));
1294        }
1295        changed
1296    }

1298    /// Margin, its uncertainty and fastest round-trip. Races are remembered for `memory` times
1299    /// margin plus spread, so the window scales with the relayer.
1300    fn judge(&self, relayer: &OffchainPublicKey, memory: u32) -> Option<Judgement> {
1301        let mut history = self.relayers.get_mut(relayer)?;
1302        let remembered = history.memory;
1303        let recent = |race: &Race| remembered.is_zero() || race.at.elapsed() <= remembered;
1304        let still_out = history.open.values().filter(|race| recent(race)).map(|race| Race {
1305            margin: race.at.elapsed().as_secs_f64(),
1306            ..*race
1307        });
1308        let races: Vec<Race> = history
1309            .races
1310            .iter()
1311            .rev()
1312            .enumerate()
1313            .take_while(|(i, race)| *i < MIN_RACES || recent(race))
1314            .map(|(_, race)| *race)
1315            .chain(still_out)
1316            .collect();
1317        if races.len() < MIN_RACES {
1318            return None;
1319        }
1320        let mut margins: Vec<f64> = races.iter().map(|race| race.margin).collect();
1321        let margin = median(&mut margins);
1322        let spread = median(&mut margins.iter().map(|m| (m - margin).abs()).collect::<Vec<_>>());
1323        history.memory = Duration::from_secs_f64(f64::from(memory) * (margin.abs() + spread));
1324        let memory = history.memory;
1325        history.forget_older_than(memory);
1326
1327        let fastest = races
1328            .iter()
1329            .map(|race| race.winning_round_trip)
1330            .fold(f64::INFINITY, f64::min);
1331        Some(Judgement {
1332            margin,
1333            uncertainty: spread / (races.len() as f64).sqrt(),
1334            fastest,
1335        })
1336    }
1337}

1341    values.sort_unstable_by(f64::total_cmp);
1342    values[(values.len() - 1) / 2]
1343}

1353/// Builds the [`PathId`] of a leg from the nodes it visits.
1354///
1355/// Returns `None` if any node is unknown to the graph or the leg is longer than a [`PathId`] can
1356/// hold -- in both cases the id would name edges the round-trip did not use, which is worse than
1357/// reporting nothing.
1358fn path_id(slots: &PathSlotResolver, nodes: impl IntoIterator<Item = OffchainPublicKey>) -> Option<PathId> {
1359    let mut id = [0u64; PATH_ID_SLOTS];
1360    let mut len = 0;
1361
1362    for node in nodes {
1363        if len == PATH_ID_SLOTS {
1364            return None;
1365        }
1366        id[len] = slots(&node)?;
1367        len += 1;
1368    }
1369
1370    (len > 1).then_some(id)
1371}

1378fn round_trip_paths(

1389    })

1435

1449        graph.record_edge::<NoPeerTelemetry, NoPathTelemetry>(MeasurableEdge::Surb(SurbTelemetry {
1450            paths,
1451            timestamp,
1452            expected,
1453            observed,
1454        }));
1455    }
1456
1457    if legs == 0 {
1458        // An idle node flushes every interval; an INFO line for nothing would bury the ones that count.
1459        tracing::trace!("surb round-trip flush tick: nothing to flush");
1460        return;
1461    }
1462
1463    {
1464        // One aggregate line per interval rather than one per pair of legs.
1465        tracing::info!(
1466            legs,
1467            expected = total_expected,
1468            observed = total_observed,
1469            "surb round-trip flush tick"
1470        );
1471    }
1472}

1474/// Re-judges relayer health, records what changed in the graph, and re-plans when it or a neighbour
1475/// changed, before writing off the SURBs of relayers judged dead. Recording precedes the re-plan, so
1476/// the re-plan already avoids them; the write-off follows it, so the refill goes over new paths.
1477///
1478/// Returns the sessions whose SURB generation the caller should advance, as reported by
1479/// [`SurbRoundTripRegistry::write_off_dead`].
1480pub async fn refresh_relayer_health<G, F>(
1481    registry: &SurbRoundTripRegistry,
1482    graph: &G,
1483    memory: u32,
1484    weight: f64,
1485    replan: impl FnOnce() -> F,
1486) -> Vec<HoprPseudonym>
1487where
1488    G: hopr_api::graph::NetworkGraphUpdate,
1489    F: std::future::Future<Output = ()>,
1490{
1491    let health = &registry.relayer_health;
1492    let changed = health.refresh(memory);
1493    // The verdicts themselves are logged where they are reached, in `refresh`.
1494    for (relayer, previous, current) in &changed {
1495        tracing::debug!(%relayer, previous, current, "relayer health changed");
1496    }
1497    // Every judged relayer on every flush, not only the changes: the graph keeps health on edges, and
1498    // an edge added since the last change would otherwise carry none.
1499    let healths: Vec<(OffchainPublicKey, f64)> = health
1500        .published
1501        .iter()
1502        .map(|entry| (*entry.key(), *entry.value()))
1503        .chain(
1504            changed
1505                .iter()
1506                .filter(|(.., current)| *current >= 1.0)
1507                .map(|(relayer, _, current)| (*relayer, *current)),
1508        )
1509        .map(|(relayer, health)| (relayer, health.powf(weight)))
1510        .collect();
1511    if !healths.is_empty() {
1512        graph.record_nodes_health(&healths);
1513    }
1514    if health.take_neighbour_change() || !changed.is_empty() {
1515        replan().await;
1516    }
1517    // Walks the whole pending map, twice when a verdict confirms; at the 256 MiB budget that is
1518    // hundreds of milliseconds, so it must not hold a runtime worker. The only failure is a full pool
1519    // refusing the task before it runs, which leaves the sessions unbumped this tick and the next
1520    // flush retries.
1521    let registry = registry.clone();
1522    hopr_utils::parallelize::cpu::spawn_blocking(move || registry.write_off_dead(), "surb_write_off_dead")
1523        .await
1524        .unwrap_or_else(|error| {
1525            tracing::warn!(%error, "SURB write-off deferred to the next flush");
1526            Vec::new()
1527        })
1528}

1530/// The invalid SURBs to hand each session this tick: every current mark, re-applied so a session
1531/// opened since picks it up, and `(0, 0.0)` for sessions whose mark has gone.
1532pub fn invalid_updates(
1533    previous: &HashMap<HoprPseudonym, (u64, f64)>,
1534    current: &HashMap<HoprPseudonym, (u64, f64)>,
1535) -> Vec<(HoprPseudonym, (u64, f64))> {
1536    current
1537        .iter()
1538        .map(|(session, invalid)| (*session, *invalid))
1539        .chain(
1540            previous
1541                .keys()
1542                .filter(|session| !current.contains_key(session))
1543                .map(|session| (*session, (0, 0.0))),
1544        )
1545        .collect()
1546}

1570impl<C> SurbTelemetryCodec<C> {
1571    /// Wraps `inner`, resolving path slots with `slots` and accumulating into `registry`.
1572    pub fn new(inner: C, me: OffchainPublicKey, slots: PathSlotResolver, registry: SurbRoundTripRegistry) -> Self {
1573        Self {
1574            inner,
1575            me,
1576            slots,
1577            registry,
1578        }
1579    }
1580
1581    /// Associates freshly minted SURBs with the legs they were minted over.
1582    ///
1583    /// Opener order is significant, so the ids zip positionally with the return paths that produced
1584    /// them.
1585    fn on_minted(&self, routing: &ResolvedTransportRouting<HoprSurb>, minted: &[HoprSurbId]) {
1586        let ResolvedTransportRouting::Forward {
1587            pseudonym,
1588            forward_path,
1589            return_paths,
1590        } = routing
1591        else {
1592            return;
1593        };
1594
1595        let forward: Vec<_> = forward_path.transport_path().iter().copied().collect();
1596        let Some(destination) = forward.last().copied() else {
1597            return;
1598        };
1599
1600        tracing::trace!(
1601            minted = minted.len(),
1602            return_paths = return_paths.len(),
1603            forward_hops = forward.len(),
1604            "surb mint seen by telemetry"
1605        );
1606
1607        let replies: Vec<Vec<OffchainPublicKey>> = return_paths
1608            .iter()
1609            .map(|return_path| return_path.transport_path().iter().copied().collect())
1610            .collect();
1611        // A reply leg is `[R, ..., me]`: everything before us is a relayer a late or missing reply
1612        // implicates. A direct reply has none.
1613        let return_relayers: Vec<ReturnRelayers> = replies
1614            .iter()
1615            .map(|reply| {
1616                let mut relayers = [None; PATH_ID_SLOTS];
1617                for (slot, relayer) in relayers.iter_mut().zip(&reply[..reply.len().saturating_sub(1)]) {
1618                    *slot = Some(*relayer);
1619                }
1620                relayers
1621            })
1622            .collect();
1623        let partners = race_partners(minted, &return_relayers);
1624        let minted_at = Instant::now();
1625
1626        for (i, surb_id) in minted.iter().enumerate().take(replies.len()) {
1627            let Some(paths) = round_trip_paths(&self.slots, &self.me, &forward, &replies[i]) else {
1628                tracing::trace!(reply_hops = replies[i].len(), "surb round-trip legs did not resolve");
1629                continue;
1630            };
1631
1632            self.registry.record_minted(paths, 1, destination);
1633            self.registry.pending.insert(
1634                *surb_id,
1635                PendingSurb {
1636                    paths,
1637                    pseudonym: *pseudonym,
1638                    return_relayers: return_relayers[i],
1639                    minted_at,
1640                    partner: partners[i],
1641                    pair_index: i % 2,
1642                    partner_replied: None,
1643                },
1644            );
1645        }
1646    }
1647
1648    /// Credits the legs a reply came back on, and settles its race once the partner's is home too.
1649    fn on_replied(&self, surb_id: &HoprSurbId) {
1650        let health = &self.registry.relayer_health;
1651        // Only a reply on a SURB we minted gets here (its opener resolved), so whatever it says about
1652        // its legs, it is the network delivering: including one whose association has gone.
1653        health.record_reply();
1654        // A SURB is single-use, so the association is consumed with it.
1655        let Some(pending) = self.registry.pending.remove(surb_id) else {
1656            // Written off because its relayer was judged dead, or wiped with its Session's stock, and
1657            // spent by the counterparty all the same: a probe through a relayer on probation arrives
1658            // like this, and so does a SURB spent before the new generation reached the counterparty.
1659            // Its legs are not credited, the expectation was written off with it, but it proves its
1660            // relayers are delivering: those it is news about (see `RelayerHealth::is_fresh_for`).
1661            if let Some((leg, minted_at)) = self.registry.written_off_legs.remove(surb_id) {
1662                health.note_home(&leg, minted_at);
1663                health.revive_by_reply(&leg, minted_at);
1664            } else {
1665                tracing::trace!("reply on a surb with no pending legs");
1666            }
1667            return;
1668        };
1669        self.registry.record_observed(pending.paths, 1);
1670
1671        // Every relayer on its leg is delivering: a dead verdict or probation on one of them ends, unless
1672        // the SURB is older than a verdict already acted on (see `RelayerHealth::is_fresh_for`).
1673        health.note_home(&pending.return_relayers, pending.minted_at);
1674        health.revive_by_reply(&pending.return_relayers, pending.minted_at);
1675        // Only a SURB the replier's pop order makes attributable settles: the other may have waited in
1676        // the replier's store.
1677        if let Some((partner_relayers, partner_home)) = pending.partner_replied {
1678            if !self.registry.judges(pending.pair_index) {
1679                return;
1680            }
1681            health.record_race(
1682                &pending.return_relayers,
1683                *surb_id,
1684                &partner_relayers,
1685                partner_home.duration_since(pending.minted_at),
1686                partner_home.elapsed(),
1687            );
1688        } else if let Some(partner) = pending.partner {
1689            // Atomic, so a partner consumed by a concurrent decode is not brought back.
1690            let home = Instant::now();
1691            let updated = self
1692                .registry
1693                .pending
1694                .entry(partner)
1695                .and_compute_with(|current| match current {
1696                    Some(current) => {
1697                        let mut waiting = current.into_value();
1698                        waiting.partner_replied = Some((pending.return_relayers, home));
1699                        moka::ops::compute::Op::Put(waiting)
1700                    }
1701                    None => moka::ops::compute::Op::Nop,
1702                });
1703            if let moka::ops::compute::CompResult::ReplacedWith(waiting) = updated {
1704                if !self.registry.judges(waiting.value().pair_index) {
1705                    return;
1706                }
1707                self.registry.open_race(
1708                    &waiting.value().return_relayers,
1709                    partner,
1710                    &pending.return_relayers,
1711                    home.duration_since(pending.minted_at),
1712                );
1713            }
1714        }
1715    }
1716}

1718/// The SURB each one races: its neighbour in mint order (first with second, third with fourth),
1719/// when both return over relayers and not over the same ones.
1720fn race_partners(minted: &[HoprSurbId], return_relayers: &[ReturnRelayers]) -> Vec<Option<HoprSurbId>> {
1721    (0..minted.len())
1722        .map(|i| {
1723            let neighbour = i ^ 1;
1724            match (return_relayers.get(i), return_relayers.get(neighbour)) {
1725                (Some(own), Some(other)) if own[0].is_some() && other[0].is_some() && own != other => {
1726                    minted.get(neighbour).copied()
1727                }
1728                _ => None,
1729            }
1730        })
1731        .collect()
1732}

1767    /// Decode a packet and record its reply SURB while keeping routine diagnostics at TRACE.
1768    fn decode(
1769        &self,
1770        sender: PeerId,
1771        data: Bytes,
1772    ) -> Result<IncomingPacket, IncomingPacketError<<Self as PacketDecoder>::Error>> {
1773        let packet = self.inner.decode(sender, data)?;
1774
1775        if let IncomingPacket::Final(f) = &packet {
1776            tracing::trace!(on_surb = f.replied_on_surb.is_some(), "final packet decoded");
1777        }
1778
1779        if let IncomingPacket::Final(final_packet) = &packet
1780            && let Some(surb_id) = final_packet.replied_on_surb
1781        {
1782            self.on_replied(&surb_id);
1783        }
1784
1785        Ok(packet)
1786    }

4685    /// A record evicted after its partner came home takes the race it is out on with it. Its reply would
4686    /// find no record and settle nothing, so the race would expire as a loss of a relayer that may well
4687    /// have delivered it, and enough of them under sustained eviction would judge that relayer dead and
4688    /// wipe its Sessions' stock (hoprnet#8624).
4689    #[test]
4690    fn an_evicted_surb_should_not_leave_its_race_open() {
4691        const CAPACITY: u8 = 8;
4692        let registry = SurbRoundTripRegistry::with_capacity(u64::from(CAPACITY) * PENDING_SURB_RECORD_BYTES);
4693        let health = registry.relayer_health();
4694        let recorder = SurbTelemetryCodec::new((), key(), no_path_slots(), registry);
4695        let pending = &recorder.registry.pending;
4696        let [late, home] = [(); 2].map(|_| key());
4697
4698        // Two pairs whose second SURB comes home first: a race is open on each first SURB, both over
4699        // the same relayer.
4700        for (first, second) in [(1, 2), (3, 4)] {
4701            pending.insert(surb_id(first), pending_to(&[late], Some(surb_id(second)), 0));
4702            pending.insert(surb_id(second), pending_to(&[home], Some(surb_id(first)), 1));
4703            recorder.on_replied(&surb_id(second));
4704        }
4705        assert_eq!(2, open_races_of(&health, &late));
4706
4707        // Newer mints fill the map, pushing out the older of the two only.
4708        for seed in 100..100 + CAPACITY - 1 {
4709            pending.insert(surb_id(seed), pending_to(&[], None, 0));
4710        }
4711        pending.run_pending_tasks();
4712        assert!(!pending.contains_key(&surb_id(1)), "the oldest record is evicted");
4713        assert!(pending.contains_key(&surb_id(3)), "the newer one is kept");
4714        assert_eq!(1, recorder.registry.pending_lost());
4715        assert_eq!(
4716            1,
4717            open_races_of(&health, &late),
4718            "the evicted record's race is discarded, the other stays open"
4719        );
4720
4721        // The evicted SURB's reply comes home on nothing and books nothing against its relayer.
4722        recorder.on_replied(&surb_id(1));
4723        assert!(margins_of(&health, &late).is_empty());
4724        assert_eq!(1, open_races_of(&health, &late));
4725    }
