Expand description

Puts one cycle’s commitment burst on the wire and, only once all of it is out, announces its deposit address.

The order is the point: the deposit must not begin before the Exit holds the complete commitment it derives the deposit key from. If a send fails the burst stops where it is and nothing is announced; the caller still owes the cycle its publication and decides what to do about that. The same goes for an announcement that cannot be queued: the Exit then holds the whole commitment, but the upper layer was never told to fund it.
