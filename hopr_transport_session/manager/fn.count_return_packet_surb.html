Counts the SURB a return packet will take as consumed in estimator, and attaches the refund that gives it back.

The Exit counts as it hands a packet on, because its reserve and admission decisions need the count at once. The packet can still be dropped before it takes a SURB: the PIX egress gate may never admit it (see acquire_egress_permit), and routing resolution may find no SURB within its wait. The refund then counts the SURB as given back in this estimator (see [SurbRefund]). It points at the estimator itself, so it reaches the Session that counted the packet whatever has happened to the Session’s slot since, and it never touches the slot.

The refund restores the held count the fill reserve and the drain budget read, and nothing else: the balancer’s level, which the egress rate and the level reports follow, saturates at zero and has already absorbed a count made while it was empty (see SurbFlowEstimator::estimate_surbs_refunded).
