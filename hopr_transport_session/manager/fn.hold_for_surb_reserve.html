Expand description

Holds an outgoing data packet back while the Exit’s SURB stock is down to its keep-alive reserve (see current_keep_alive_surb_reserve).

Returns None, with nothing allocated, in the common case of a stock above the reserve. Otherwise the future resolves once the stock has risen above it, polling the estimate every SURB_RESERVE_POLL. It has no deadline of its own: the Session’s own deadlines apply, and a Session that is closed drops the future with the sink.

Only once the Entry has shown that it refills the stock (BalancerStateValues::counterparty_refills): that is, a keep-alive from its balancer, which only an Entry with a SURB balancer sends (a manual ping, KeepAliveFlag::ManualPing, is not one). An Entry without one can run a rate-controlled Session too: it announces no target (one that predates this announces the balancer-rate fallback, which the Exit cannot tell from a real target), attaches one SURB to each data packet and sends no keep-alives but the odd manual ping. So the announcement says nothing about a balancer either way. A reply held for a stock above the reserve would then wait until the Entry sent that many more packets.

The Exit’s keep-alives do not come through here: its keep-alive stream sends on the message sender directly (see SessionManager::spawn_exit_keep_alive), and the PIX fill packets are bounded by their own min_surb_reserve. That is the point of the reserve: they are what it is held for.
