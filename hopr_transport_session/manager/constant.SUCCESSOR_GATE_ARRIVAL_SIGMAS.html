Standard deviations below an honest Exit’s expected returned packets at which the successor gate sets its bar — see successor_required_packets.

Four put the chance that an Exit losing exactly the tolerated share fraction falls short of the bar at 3 × 10⁻⁵, and that it falls short by more than the near-miss window — refused without waiting — at about one in 1.5 million at the deployed split (normal approximation). A shortfall inside the window still gets SSA_SUCCESSOR_SERVICE_WAIT for the Session’s ongoing traffic to make it up. The price is ~0.33 % of the boundary added to what an Exit can withhold and still be funded: the deployed bar moves from 41.3 % of a cycle’s packets to 41.0 %.
