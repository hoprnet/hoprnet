Expand description

The fraction of the best candidate’s (tempered) weight below which a candidate is one the cost function all but rejects.

A relayer on probation is published at a health of a thousandth and an edge probes measured dead is floored at a billionth: tempered, they sit at about 3 % and 0.003 % of a healthy candidate. A stalled relayer still delivering at a hundredth of its health is at 10 %, and stays explorable.

Those figures are for the default return_path_weight_temper of 0.5. A weight tempered by t puts a relayer on probation at 0.001^t of a healthy one, so a temper of 1/3 or less lifts it to this fraction or above: exploration then counts it as plausible, forward draws among them, and the probe budget no longer holds it back. Whoever lowers the temper that far trades this gate for the wider spread.
