The fewest overdue replies that make a relayer a black hole over one of its hops while it delivers over the other (see RaceHistory::hop_at_fault).

More than MIN_RACES. That it delivers at all says it is alive, so what is left to judge is one hop, on half its replies, and a verdict over one hop is acted on once that hop has been silent for a flush, which a hop carrying little is anyway. With replies lost at random, MIN_RACES in a row over one hop then come about by chance. Simulated on this code with every relayer healthy and 5 or 10 % of the replies lost: with return paths drawn at random among 30 relayers, 13 verdicts acted on with three against 3 with four; with the planner’s sets of eight paths it makes no difference (5 against 4). It is margin, and cheap: on a cluster a relayer cut off from the replier has a median of 9 replies overdue at the flush that first suspects it.
