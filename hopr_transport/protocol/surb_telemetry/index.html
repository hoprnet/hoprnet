Module surb_telemetry 

Source
Expand description

Records SURB round-trips as network graph edge telemetry. Turns SURB round-trips into edge telemetry for the network graph.

A SURB rides a forward path to reach its destination and carries a return path for the reply to come back on. When the reply arrives, that is proof both legs passed end to end – evidence the graph already wants, produced by traffic a session was sending anyway. Unlike a probe it costs no extra packets and accrues at data rates, which is what lets a dead relayer be noticed in seconds rather than after a probe success rate has moved behind a path cache.

§Why one layer owns both directions

Minting and consuming happen at opposite ends of the pipeline, but only together do they mean anything: the mint says what was expected, the reply says what was observed. Wrapping both codec halves keeps the SURB-to-path association private to this module, so nothing below has to carry a path identity it has no other use for.

§Mitigations driven from here

Each names its trigger, its effect, its setting and what it must not work against.

  • Relayer health. Trigger: a relayer losing reply races (two SURBs of one packet, different return paths) by more than a round trip. Effect: its health in the graph scales every edge into it, so forward and return paths through it are drawn less. Settings: HoprProtocolConfig::latency_race_memory, HoprProtocolConfig::relayer_health_weight (0 off). Must not fight: the return-path temper, which flattens it along with path quality.
  • Dead verdict. Trigger: at least MIN_RACES replies trailing their partners by more than any race the relayer settled, none home since, and a reply home through some relayer after the first of them fell due. A reply is overdue only while others are delivering: a pause in the replier’s stream, or an outage of every return relayer, leaves nothing arriving and so nothing overdue, however long the replies stay out. A relayer that has settled too few races to have a margin of its own borrows the bar of the best-measured relayer, raised to whatever its own settled races measure, and none is judged while no relayer has one; its own races that expired open are booked as lost and are not replies that came home, so they do not reset the clock. Effect: health 0, the walk prunes it. A verdict is lifted by a reply coming home through the relayer, not by time. Once no reply through it is out any more (about PENDING_SURB_TTL) the condition lapses, and the relayer goes on probation: published at PROBATION_HEALTH, which weighted selection all but never draws while the planner’s exploration still does, and that draw is the probe. A probe reply home through it revives it, to the health its lost races leave it, so gradually; a probe that goes missing leaves it where it is, and one that is a black hole again goes back to dead without a new SURB generation. Must not fight: common-mode outages, where no partner gets home and nothing is judged.
  • Invalid SURBs. Trigger: a dead verdict, or probation. Effect: each session is told how many SURBs in its counterparty’s store run through that relayer; the balancer subtracts them from its level and sends replacements. A lapsed verdict is only a timeout and the counterparty still holds the SURBs, probe SURBs included, so the write-off outlives it; each written-off SURB counts as held only until WRITE_OFF_HORIZON, the time the counterparty needs to spend it, and a reply through the relayer (which ends the probation) or the end of the session ends the write-off sooner. A session’s count is dropped when its SURB generation is advanced, though: the wipe takes the SURBs it counted, and from then on it counts only the probes minted since. Must not fight: flow control, which keeps the raw level so the replacements are not throttled.
  • Fresh SURB generation. Trigger: a dead verdict that held for a second flush (VERDICT_CONFIRMATION) reaching a session for the first time, at most once per (session, relayer) while the relayer is known dead, and per REBUMP_COOLDOWN after it is revived. Effect: the session’s SURB generation advances, so the counterparty drops its whole stock on its next push, the dead SURBs it cannot recognise along with the rest, and the balancer refills from empty, paced; the session’s write-off goes with the stock it counted. Probation is not a new verdict: a pair bumped in this death is not bumped again until a reply revives the relayer, so one that dies again on probation costs the session nothing more. Only stock advances a generation: a SURB minted after its relayer was already known dead is a probe, written off without one, also in a session the wipe never reached. Must not fight: the cooldown, since each advance costs the counterparty everything it holds, and a PIX cycle can absorb only so many such losses.
  • Lost neighbour. Trigger: our own connection drops. Effect: re-plan only; the graph’s connection status already rejects that edge, so its SURBs are not invalidated.
