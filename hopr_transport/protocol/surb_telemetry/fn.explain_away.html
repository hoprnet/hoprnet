Of the relayers whose overdue replies would make them black holes, those whose lateness no other one explains.

A reply out late counts against every relayer on its leg that its partner’s leg does not share, since a race cannot tell which of them held it. With a leg’s relayers drawn from many, the relayers on legs through a dead one collected its overdue replies too, and were judged dead with it: on a cluster, an innocent relayer was judged dead in two of every three runs that killed one (48 of 72) once the paths were spread over every first relayer, against one in four before.

A dead relayer is on all the legs that failed through it, an innocent one only on those it shares with it, so the dead one has the most. They are taken in that order: one is a black hole if at least MIN_RACES of its unanswered replies are on legs through no relayer already taken, and every reply out on a leg through it is then explained. Two relayers dead at once are both taken while each has replies the other does not explain; one that only ever sat on legs through the other cannot be told from an innocent one, and waits until those legs are gone.

The fewest relayers that explain the overdue replies are taken, so the reverse also holds: a live relayer whose every leg ran through relayers that died collects all their overdue replies, more than any of them has, and is taken in their place, one dead relayer explaining the lateness as well as two. That takes a live relayer with nothing delivered through it since: one that also sits on a leg that delivers has its overdue replies answered (see RaceHistory::unanswered) and is no suspect. Ties of equal counts go by key.
