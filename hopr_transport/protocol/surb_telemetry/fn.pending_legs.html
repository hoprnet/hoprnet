Builds the shared pending map, bounded by a byte capacity, a per-record weight and a TTL.

max_capacity is in bytes, matched by the weigher that charges every record PENDING_SURB_RECORD_BYTES, so the budget is the map’s real bound.

Admission is lru, not moka’s default TinyLFU: a SURB is looked up once, when its reply comes home, so every key’s frequency is about one and TinyLFU refuses the newest record once the map is full. A refused record settles no race and, if the SURB is lost, is never written off (hoprnet#8624).

Under lru the newest is always admitted and the victim is the oldest, whose reply comes next under FIFO — the cost the issue names. It is bounded by the budget while outstanding SURBs fit: a record survives capacity / mint rate, and a reply comes home after the counterparty has spent its stock (minutes, which is why PENDING_SURB_HORIZON is 15 minutes). Once the outstanding total exceeds the capacity — above ~16 fully-buffered Sessions at the default — residency falls below that wait, so an ever larger share of records is evicted before their replies arrive, and one high-rate Session shortens the residence time for every other. That regime is reachable by design and is counted via SurbRoundTripRegistry::pending_lost; raising pending_surb_max_bytes extends the range over which eviction is not the normal state.

A record evicted while its partner is home leaves the race that partner opened with nothing to close it: the reply finds no record, settles nothing, and the race would expire as a loss of the relayers on its leg, healthy or not. Under sustained eviction that is enough unanswered races to judge a healthy relayer dead and wipe its Sessions’ stock. An eviction is no evidence about a relayer either way, so the race is discarded with the record (see SurbRoundTripRegistry::open_race for the eviction that lands before the race opens).
